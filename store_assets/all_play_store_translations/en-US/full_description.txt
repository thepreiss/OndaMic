OndaMic — Transform your Android smartphone into a professional live pocket microphone!

Need to teach a class, give a presentation, lead an event, make announcements, or sing karaoke without a dedicated microphone? OndaMic transforms your phone into a versatile live microphone for external speakers via 3.5mm Aux (P2), USB-C, or Bluetooth — 100% free, zero ads, and 100% offline.

🌟 Key Features:
• Clear Call Mode (Voice Focus) & Studio Low-Latency Mode
• Mono & True Dual-Mic Stereo (Left + Right separated channels)
• Select Physical Microphone Capsule & Polar Direction (Front, Back, 360°)
• Intelligent Anti-Feedback Shield: Auto-Notch filter & 180° phase flip
• Vocal Effects: Brightness, Stage Reverb, Echo Delay & 5-Band Equalizer
• Stage Ready: Push-to-Talk (screen & volume keys), VOX, Cough/Mute button & Chime
• 100% Offline & Private: No Internet permission. Your voice never leaves your device.
